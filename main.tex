\label{sec:background}
\noindent
A mass distribution deflects light, the telescope records a blurred and noisy image, and a shape estimator reduces that image to measured quantities. A convergence map connects these quantities back to the projected mass through a calibrated statistical estimate of the lensing distortion~\cite{bartelmann_weak_2001,mandelbaum_weak_2018}.
